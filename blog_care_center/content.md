## Optima’s CARE – The Center for Animal Research and Excellence
Some partnerships go beyond business — they build trust, inspire learning, and move an entire industry forward.
As an Optima customer, I’ve experienced this spirit firsthand. Whether it’s a breakthrough nutritional formulation, a bold trial on disease resilience, or a new approach to animal welfare — the Discovery Research projects undertaken by CARE have been nothing short of thrilling. Each of them are solutions that blend curiosity and practical impact, making a real difference on our farms and for our business.
![Optima CARE inauguration](https://www.optimalife.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/565415997_18083593433497721_7237407578225202030_n-1.jpeg)
What truly sets CARE apart are its guiding values — **Curiosity** , driving innovation through relentless inquiry; **Application** , turning research into real-world solutions; **Respect** , honoring the dignity of animals and people; and **Excellence** , committing to the highest scientific rigor in everything they do. These values create a foundation where science meets deep care.
![Optima CARE inauguration](https://www.optimalife.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/564587177_18083593451497721_358934642814285844_n.jpeg)
![Optima CARE inauguration](https://www.optimalife.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/565400987_18083593463497721_1256512767334726326_n.jpeg)
Every interaction, every collaboration, and every conversation with Optima has reflected not just innovation, but genuine intention — the will to advance animal health and welfare through science.
![Optima CARE inauguration](https://www.optimalife.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/564215834_18083593475497721_5453096839043105965_n.jpg)
![Optima CARE inauguration](https://www.optimalife.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/565176520_18083593484497721_7984516405769263407_n.jpeg)
Today, as **Dr. Sudheer Rukadikar** opened the doors of **CARE** — the **Center for Animal Research and Excellence** — that intention felt more real than ever. CARE isn’t just a facility; it represents a vision coming alive — where research meets compassion, and innovation feels deeply human.
![Optima CARE inauguration](https://www.optimalife.in/care-center-for-animal-research-and-excellence/)
As someone who has witnessed Optima’s journey closely, I can say this with conviction: when purpose meets expertise, transformation isn’t just inevitable — it’s already begun.
_— Words from one of Optima’s trusted partners._
