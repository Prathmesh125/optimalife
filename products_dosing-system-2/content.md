## XylamaxR
Optima introduces extensive range of high performance liquid dosing system having highest quality standards which is extensively used in poultry feed process industry where reliability, accuracy and long term performance are essential.  
It is an innovative, industry leading liquid dosing technology serving global environment and working closely with industry. Our specialist advisers combine world class technical expertise with our own advanced dosing technologies to create practical solution to resolve complex problems of feed industry and fully supported by experienced engineering team.
