## Advancing Poultry Nutrition and Technology with Optima Life Sciences at Dubai Poultry Technology & Innovation Symposium 2025 
Optima Life Sciences recently participated in the Dubai Poultry Technology & Innovation Symposium 2025 in collaboration with WPSA. This was an invaluable platform for meaningful collaboration within the poultry sectors globally. 
[ ](https://www.optimalife.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/WPSA-Dubai-Poultry-Symposium-OLS-1.jpg) [ ](https://www.optimalife.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/WPSA-Dubai-Poultry-Symposium-OLS-2.jpg) [ ](https://www.optimalife.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/WPSA-Dubai-Poultry-Symposium-OLS-3.jpg) [ ](https://www.optimalife.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/WPSA-Dubai-Poultry-Symposium-OLS-4.jpg) [ ](https://www.optimalife.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/WPSA-Dubai-Poultry-Symposium-OLS-5.jpg) [ ](https://www.optimalife.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/WPSA-Dubai-Poultry-Symposium-OLS-6.jpg)
One of the most outstanding moments of the event was that of a valuable and insightful session by Mr. Vinay Kulkarni, Executive Chairman, of Optima Life Sciences. His session was regarding the topic of “Collaborative Approaches to Technology Adoption in Poultry.” His session was truly insightful leading to some thought-provoking discussion with leading key poultry leaders. The purpose of the event was to highlight the importance of technology and embrace it to the benefit of poultry production.
