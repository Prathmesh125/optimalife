# Optima Life Sciences Acquires Cure Medicines India Pvt. Ltd.
  * [ Home ](https://www.optimalife.in)
  * Our Blog


![](https://www.optimalife.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/cure-medicines-acquisition-1.jpg)
## There comes a point when growth is no longer about doing more.
It becomes about becoming more.
More capable. More relevant. More prepared for the future.
Today, we are proud to announce the acquisition of Cure Medicines India Pvt. Ltd., a strategic step that strengthens Optima’s pharmaceutical capabilities and accelerates our long-term vision.
![Cure Medicines acquisition](https://www.optimalife.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/cure-medicines-acquisition-2.jpg)  
![Cure Medicines acquisition](https://www.optimalife.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/cure-medicines-acquisition-3.jpg)
We were honoured to mark this milestone alongside Dr. Sudipto Haldar, Director – Research, Agrivet, as our chief guest, whose insights reinforced the importance of science, innovation, and continuous evolution.
![Cure Medicines acquisition](https://www.optimalife.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/cure-medicines-acquisition-4.jpg)  
![Cure Medicines acquisition](https://www.optimalife.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/cure-medicines-acquisition-5.jpg)
This acquisition is not the destination.
It is the foundation for what comes next.
