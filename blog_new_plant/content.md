# Inauguration of Optima Life Sciences’ New Manufacturing Plant
  * [ Home ](https://www.optimalife.in)
  * Our Blog


![](https://www.optimalife.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/inauguration_otima_life_day2_08.jpg)
We are thrilled to announce the inauguration of our cutting-edge manufacturing plant in Jejuri, Maharashtra, ushering in a new era for Optima Life Sciences in poultry health and production. The celebration, held on 15th October 2024, featured insightful presentations from Dr. Nivedita Pande and Dr. Uday Patel, attracting over 150 industry professionals. Together, we are committed to enhancing poultry health and production for a sustainable future.
